About us

Why we built
The Household Ledger.

We built The Household Ledger after watching this pattern play out in our own lives and the lives of the people around us: one parent quietly carrying the mental weight of the household while it goes largely unseen, until exhaustion and resentment start to erode the relationship itself. The imbalance isn't a personal failing — it's a structural one, tied to real consequences: burnout, strained partnerships, and parents who feel like they're disappearing into their own household. Left unaddressed, that silent, growing weight doesn't stay contained to the person carrying it — it wears down the relationship around it. We built this to make the invisible visible.
